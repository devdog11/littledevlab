## About section
**Built by someone who couldn't find what they needed, so they made it.**

LittleDevLab started the way most good projects do — out of frustration.
The glasses and contacts holder came first: an attempt to clear counter
clutter without buying a plastic organizer that almost-fit. One model, one
print, one problem solved.

Every piece is designed, printed, and quality-checked in the SF Bay Area
before it ships. No downloaded templates, no mass-production shortcuts —
just a small lab figuring out real household problems one print at a time.

See the full design process and build log at [littledevlab.com](https://littledevlab.com).

## Featured listing: Glasses + Contact Lens Holder
**Title:** Glasses + Contact Lens Holder — Daily Lens Organizer, 3D Printed

**Description:**
Tired of morning contact lens chaos? Start your day with complete clarity —
no more fumbling in the dark, squinting at lens boxes, or accidentally
ripping foil covers on your daily lens strips. This organizer streamlines
your routine so you can grab your lenses effortlessly and get on with your
day.

Designed for ultimate convenience, the modular system lets you
pre-separate up to 15 days of lenses ahead of time when you're not in a
rush — whether you wear standard dual-prescription lenses or need three
distinct prescriptions, the 2- and 3-compartment configurations keep every
lens organized and within reach.

- Pre-separated storage — holds up to 15 days of daily lenses per compartment
- Flexible prescriptions — 2-compartment and 3-compartment models
- Curved glasses cradle add-on — rests eyewear safely without stressing hinges
- Versatile mounting — under-cabinet or countertop, with add-on slots for
  toothbrushes, razors, and other essentials
- Colors: Warm White, Matte Black, Light Gray, or Custom
- Designed, printed, and quality-checked in the SF Bay Area
