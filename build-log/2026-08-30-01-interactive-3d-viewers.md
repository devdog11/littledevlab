Every product block now embeds a `<model-viewer>` 3D preview alongside black/white color-variant renders, and the page went from static markup to dynamic (variant chips swap images/renders in place). This is effectively a manual, one-off version of the pattern the Folder-Driven Product Site Generator pilot (Section 12) is meant to eventually automate.
